# Logo Usage Guide

## Primary Mark
- Use the SopySafe mark on dark or light backgrounds with sufficient breathing room.

## Clear Space
- Leave at least one logo-height of space around the mark.

## Color Rules
- Prefer emerald or white treatment depending on background contrast.
- Do not stretch, skew, or recolor the brand mark with unapproved colors.

## Minimum Size
- Keep the full logo readable on mobile headers and social preview images.
